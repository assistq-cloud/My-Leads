FAQ

How is this different from a contact form?

A form just collects data — AssistQ actually has a conversation, answers questions in real time, and only sends you leads once they're genuinely qualified. Higher-quality leads, fewer people abandoning the page.

Can I get SEO Growth on its own?

SEO Growth now ships bundled inside the AI Chatbot Suite rather than as a standalone plan — so audits, monthly fixes, and reporting are already included the moment your chatbot goes live, at one combined price.

What's in the dashboard?

Every Chatbot Suite client gets a login to track keyword rankings, review SEO audit scores, see which UTM sources bring hot leads, and edit your chatbot's greeting and qualifying questions — all without waiting on us.

What happens after I get a lead?

You're notified instantly by email with a one-tap WhatsApp link, so you can follow up the moment someone's interested — no waiting, no missed opportunities.

How fast can you set this up?

Typically within 48 hours of our initial conversation about your business and FAQ content.
