Questions, answered plainly

Know what you're buying.

What does the setup fee cover?

It covers the initial onboarding and configuration work required to prepare your AssistQ system for your business. Any separate optional service is shown before payment.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Features are structured by plan, so a business can move to a higher plan when it needs more capability.

Can I use AssistQ with an existing website?

Yes. The lead responder can be deployed on an existing website. If you do not have a website, an optional hosted landing page is currently priced at ₹2,999 one-time.

Does AssistQ replace my sales team?

No. AssistQ is designed to give the team a better workflow for lead capture, qualification, follow-up and visibility.

How is my information handled?

We aim to collect only data needed to respond to requests, provide the service, process payment and meet legal obligations. See the Privacy Policy for details.
