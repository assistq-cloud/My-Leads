A form just collects data — AssistQ actually has a conversation, answers questions in real time, and only sends you leads once they're genuinely qualified. Higher-quality leads, fewer people abandoning the page.

Yes — it runs on the same tier of AI technology major companies use, and every lead is automatically logged and backed up. We handle setup and maintenance, so there's zero technical effort required from you.

What happens after I get a lead?

You're notified instantly by email with a one-tap WhatsApp link, so you can follow up the moment someone's interested — no waiting, no missed opportunities.

How fast can you set this up?

Typically within 48 hours of our initial conversation about your business and FAQ content.

No — if you don't have one, we deliver your bot as a standalone shareable link you can add to your Google Business Profile, WhatsApp, or a QR code.
