1. Who we are

AssistQ is a product of SonQAI Technologies ("AssistQ", "we", "us", "our"). We provide an AI chatbot, lead-qualification, and CRM dashboard service for small and mid-sized businesses (referred to below as our "business clients"), and we operate the chatbot on behalf of those business clients when their website visitors or prospective customers ("visitors" or "leads") interact with it.

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what choices you have — whether you're a visitor chatting with one of our clients' chatbots, a business client using our dashboard, or a prospective client going through checkout on this site.

2. What information we collect

What we collect depends on how you interact with AssistQ:

If you are...We collect
A visitor chatting with a chatbot (on a client's website or one of our hosted landing pages)Name, phone number, email, and whatever you tell the chatbot to answer its questions (e.g. budget, timeline, requirement, business type). We also automatically capture the conversation transcript, a lead score we calculate, and campaign/UTM parameters from the link you arrived on.
A business client using our dashboardYour business name, contact email and phone, login access code, and whatever business data you enter or connect — leads, sales pipeline stages, team member details, project/inventory listings, documents you link (not upload — see below), commission records, and any Google or WhatsApp accounts you choose to connect.
A prospective client checking out on this siteFull name, business name, email, and phone, which we pass to our payment processor (Razorpay) to set up your subscription. We do not see or store your card, UPI, or bank details — those go directly to Razorpay.
Note on the "Document Vault" dashboard feature: it stores a link to a document you already have elsewhere (e.g. Google Drive) plus a label and status — we do not host or store the file itself.

3. How we use your information

  • To operate the chatbot conversation and respond to your questions in real time
  • To score and route leads to the correct business client, and notify them by email and/or WhatsApp
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the dashboard and its features for business clients
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions (setup fees, monthly billing, trials)
  • To send service communications — welcome emails, weekly performance reports, subscription/billing notices
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or misuse of the service
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information to third parties, and we do not use visitor conversation data to train third-party AI models beyond what's needed to generate the chatbot's own replies in that conversation.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that make AssistQ work, and with the business client a conversation belongs to:

  • The relevant business client — a lead's contact details, conversation, and score are always shared with the specific business whose chatbot generated that lead.
  • Razorpay — processes subscription payments. Razorpay's own privacy policy governs how they handle payment details.
  • Google (Search Console, Analytics, Gmail) — only if a business client explicitly connects their Google account, to power SEO/analytics features and email report delivery.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Cloud API — used to deliver WhatsApp notifications and template messages on behalf of business clients who enable this feature.
  • Railway — hosts our dashboard application and database, where lead, conversation, and business-client data is stored.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts our public marketing website (this site). Like any web host, it processes standard request data (e.g. IP address, browser type) to serve pages; it does not receive chatbot conversation or lead data.

We do not share personal information with advertisers, and we do not sell personal information.

5. How long we keep information

We keep lead and conversation data for as long as the relevant business client's account is active, so they can continue following up with their leads and reviewing past performance. If a business client's subscription ends, we retain their data for a reasonable period in case they reactivate, after which it may be deleted. You can ask us to delete your own personal information sooner — see Section 8.

6. How we protect information

We use industry-standard measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed/access-code-based authentication rather than storing plain passwords where practical, and role-based access so business clients only see their own data. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Our dashboard uses a session cookie to keep you logged in — this is essential to the service and isn't used for advertising or cross-site tracking. Our marketing site and chatbot may read UTM parameters from the URL you arrive on to understand which campaign brought a visitor in; this isn't a cookie, and no advertising cookies are set by AssistQ itself.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. If you're in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you rights over your personal data, including the right to access and the right to erasure (subject to legal and legitimate business retention needs).

To exercise any of these rights — whether you're a visitor who chatted with one of our clients' bots, or a business client — contact us using the details in Section 11. If your request concerns a specific business's chatbot, we may need to confirm the request with that business, since they are the ones who commissioned the chatbot and own the resulting lead relationship.

9. Children's information

AssistQ's chatbots and dashboard are intended for business use and for adults inquiring about products or services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services evolve. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do. Continued use of AssistQ after a change means you accept the updated policy.
