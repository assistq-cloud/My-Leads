Accessibility

Designed to be usable, not just impressive.

AssistQ's public site is built with semantic landmarks, keyboard-focus indicators, a skip link, readable labels, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support. Decorative effects do not carry essential information, and cursor effects are disabled on touch devices and when reduced motion is preferred.
